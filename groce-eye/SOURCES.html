Sources & licenses

groce-eye is an independent static viewer inspired by God's Eye View. It does not expose Jason's credentials or configure paid providers. The interface is original; the globe libraries have their own licenses.

Browser connections

  • Fintraffic / Digitraffic public road-weather camera locations, Finland. CC BY 4.0. Image capture may precede fetch time; updates are usually around ten minutes.
  • USGS earthquakes, last 24 hours. Public government observations, refreshed every five minutes.
  • CelesTrak GP orbital elements. Positions are calculated with SGP4; cached for at least two hours per usage guidance.
  • NOAA/NWS contiguous-US radar WMS composite reflectivity. Imagery is periodically refreshed, and may be delayed or unavailable.
  • Day/night lighting uses the current time and calculated solar position. It is not a live weather observation.
  • GeoNames cities over 15,000 population, downloaded October 11, 2026. CC BY 4.0. Names and coordinates are bundled for city search, which does not transmit your search to a geocoding provider.

Additional free connections

  • Caltrans CWWP2: documented no-charge camera-location data. Camera maps open on the official site. Third-party imagery is excluded. Feed documentation and conditions.
  • DriveBC / Government of British Columbia: Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – British Columbia. Only eligible government HighwayCam entries are included. Catalog is bundled; images come directly from DriveBC.
  • NOAA/NWS US forecasts and active alerts. Forecast periods are not live weather measurements; no-alert status is unknown if the alert feed fails.
  • NASA EONET global natural events: latest point geometry for open events reported within 30 days, up to 100 events. Incomplete and sometimes delayed; not a comprehensive emergency-warning feed.
  • NASA GIBS VIIRS SNPP corrected-reflectance daily imagery for yesterday. Clouds are visible in the mosaic. It is not live footage and may have gaps.
  • Seattle Fire Department public 911 dispatch records: public-domain official dataset; latest 150 records. Seattle coverage only, Seattle local time. Coordinates rounded to about 1 km, addresses excluded. Dispatch categories are preliminary and do not establish patient condition or incident outcomes.
  • DeFlock: external link to reported OpenStreetMap ALPR locations, not access to camera footage.

The camera coverage directory lists all 50 states. A link-only or unverified entry does not imply camera data is absent; direct embedding is held until a usable feed and reuse terms are established. Camera catalogs were acquired October 11, 2026; selected images may update separately. No continuous camera recording or automatic image archiving occurs.

Indiana official video links

TrafficWise’s public list and per-camera media links open without login. groce-eye links to its official player in a separate browser window; it does not host or embed the video. TrafficWise returns X-Frame-Options: DENY and Content-Security-Policy: frame-ancestors none, so its web player cannot appear inside a groce-eye frame. The separate developer XML feed requirement does not apply to ordinary public viewing.

Official location catalogs

Indiana: 749 public metadata entries acquired October 11, 2026 from the same anonymous GraphQL list request used by TrafficWise. 748 official point locations are plotted; one official longitude lies outside Indiana and is withheld without guessing a correction. Its video link remains available from the layer panel. Camera markers open a filtered official camera list, including the current media view when matched. Camera identifiers can change; unmatched views use the search list. Three individual camera requests independently matched the catalog coordinates and view IDs.

Nevada: 652 public-directory entries acquired October 11, 2026 from the anonymous camera-list request used by Nevada 511. Coordinates come from provider WGS84 geometry; 372 records are classified as Las Vegas. Markers open the provider’s exact camera map permalink. 628 entries have a video flag (358 in Las Vegas); the other entries offer still views. Flags are a metadata snapshot, not a live availability guarantee. No images or stream addresses are copied into these location catalogs. A Las Vegas video was observed playing without login during browser verification.

Map imagery

Welcome-page illustration: NASA Blue Marble 2012, Eastern Hemisphere composite image, NASA/GSFC. Original NASA image and description. This preview is a composite illustration, not a live globe screenshot.

World Imagery tiles: Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics, and the GIS User Community. Provider attribution remains on the map. Bundled fallback: Natural Earth II, supplied with CesiumJS. No Cesium ion or Google Maps token is configured. Satellite imagery is a basemap, not live video.

Desktop-only connections

ADSB.lol aircraft observations (ODbL 1.0), for personal exploration subject to current provider conditions. The local helper handles browser connection restrictions. Additional active-satellite records come from CelesTrak. Anonymous OpenSky is an optional local endpoint; its free access is limited and noncommercial. The viewer does not display an unavailable ship layer or require an AISStream key.

Additional US cameras

14,150 official locations across 12 additional states. Coordinates are publisher WGS84 metadata; camera media plays on official external sites. Counts describe directory snapshots and are not a guarantee of live feeds. Other states remain official map links.

Libraries

CesiumJS 1.134.1: Apache 2.0 license. satellite.js 6.0.1: MIT license. Cesium includes third-party materials and associated notices distributed with its package.

Privacy and availability

Public feeds and imagery are requested from their providers, which receive the visitor's public IP. There is no account system, analytics, microphone permission, or location permission in groce-eye. Orbital/camera metadata may be cached locally to reduce provider requests. No secret keys are bundled. Public data coverage, limits, and availability vary; layer failures are shown rather than replaced with invented live activity.

Satellite history and map additions

Maryland camera locations and viewer links: Maryland iMAP / MDOT CHART, retrieved November 15, 2026. Cameras open on the official CHART site.

Alabama, Colorado, Delaware, Kentucky and Ohio location samples are from OpenTrafficCamMap, copyright AidanWelch, MIT license. These are community-collected snapshots, not independently verified current locations. Markers link to official state maps rather than camera streams.

Starlink tracking uses public orbital elements distributed by CelesTrak via the Watched catalog. Positions are calculated estimates; the display shows up to 200 objects nearest the view center. It is not a live SpaceX telemetry feed or a prediction of internet coverage. Orbit data older than 72 hours is rejected.

Oregon camera locations use a snapshot from God’s Eye View and are credited to Oregon Department of Transportation / TripCheck and the relevant partner operators. Markers open the official TripCheck map; the specific camera must be selected there.

Iowa and Hawaii camera location samples are from traffic-camera-sources / LiveTrafficCam, credited to Iowa DOT and FAA WeatherCams respectively. Iowa links open the 511 map near the location; Hawaii links open FAA weather-camera views. Catalog snapshots may be incomplete or outdated.

Washington camera locations from Washington State Department of Transportation Travel Center. Markers open official WSDOT camera views. Catalog dated November 15, 2026; availability varies.

Satellite history uses code adapted from Watched by Armaan Gupta, under the MIT license. Its third-party notices apply. Satellite orbits use CelesTrak element sets; Sentinel imagery contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data, and Landsat imagery is courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey and NASA. NASA GIBS imagery is part of EOSDIS. Archive searches use Microsoft Planetary Computer and Element 84. Imagery is historical and passes are estimates.

State outlines: U.S. Census Bureau 2024 cartographic boundary files, generalized for display. Roads: Esri World Transportation and its data providers. Optional traffic: TomTom, subject to your API account terms and usage limits; selecting traffic sends your key and map tile requests to TomTom.

Camera links open official provider websites. For catalogs without an individual camera page, the official camera map opens and the camera name is provided for searching.
