Sources & how this works

No account, API keys, or paid services. Data travels directly between your browser and public providers. No location permission is requested.

Fintraffic / Digitraffic · CC BY 4.0 (Finland cameras), USGS, CelesTrak, NOAA/NWS, GeoNames. Imagery credits appear on the globe.

Satellite positions are calculated from orbital elements, not live measurements. Camera images usually update about every 10 minutes. Public feed availability and coverage vary.

Independent viewer inspired by God’s Eye View. Get the original project separately to configure your own optional keys.

Viewer: CesiumJS (Apache 2.0), satellite.js (MIT). Connection and license notes.

Your window on the world

Drag to rotate. Pinch or use + and − to zoom. On desktop, right-drag to tilt. Select a marker or use the camera list to open details.

Try Cameras in Finland, then pick an orange point. Earthquakes cover the last 24 hours. Satellite markers show calculated positions.

On phones, close the layer panel to see the whole globe. Camera details appear at the bottom. Full screen is optional.

Keyboard: Tab reaches the controls. Escape closes panels. Reset view returns to Earth. Share copies a view link; if clipboard access is unavailable, a selectable link appears.
