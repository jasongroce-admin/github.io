Sources & privacy

No account, API keys, or paid services. Public providers receive your internet requests. No location permission is requested.

Satellite positions are calculated estimates. Camera catalogs are dated snapshots; capture times and feed availability vary. No recording is performed by groce-eye.

Your window on the world

Drag to rotate. Pinch or use + and − to zoom. On desktop, right-drag to tilt. Select a marker to see its source or open an official camera.

Layers turns connections on and off. Cameras finds nearby views. Places searches cities and checks US weather. Settings changes or shares your view.

Planes opens aircraft controls. The free Windows package supplies the aircraft connection; the public browser edition links to that download.

Tab reaches controls. Within the tool tabs, use arrow keys, Home or End. Escape closes panels. On a phone, close Explore for the whole globe.
