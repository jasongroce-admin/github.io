Night Missions

Night battles hide the arena until shots, flares, fire, or support weapons light it up. Those flashes are warm white/yellow light, so with Night Vision on they can bloom and blow out the bright center like a real intensified view.

Use the Menu button during play to find the Night Vision toggle. Turn it on to scan the battlefield, then expect nearby explosions and flames to flare bright.
