Range. Angle. Send it.

Choose your tank and matching cockpit from MISSION MENU before deployment. Drag either dial to set elevation or charge; elevation can be fine-adjusted by a tenth of a degree. Tap a shell on the full-width rack to change the active tank's ordnance, use the track buttons to reposition, and press the red firing button. Each tank keeps its own weapon, charge and optical view; changing your shell never changes the enemy's loadout. A zero-degree elevation is a level shot; increase the angle to clear the ridge. Full charge is tuned to reach across the field with every ordnance; wind still bends shells. Impacts reshape the soil and can uncover firing lanes.

Each crew has a distinct cockpit and chassis: M40 Line has 100 armor, 180 m track reserve and 15% stronger scenery impacts. R12 Scout has 90 armor and 240 m reserve. B76 Bulwark has 115 armor, 120 m reserve and takes 10% less direct-hit damage. S90 Siege has 95 armor, 144 m reserve, 10% stronger shell / heavy direct hits and 20% stronger scenery impacts. Reserve refills at the start of that crew's next turn. The CPU follows the same rules.
