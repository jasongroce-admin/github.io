I’m Jason Groce. I like figuring things out, making things work better, and seeing what happens when “I wonder if…” becomes something you can actually try.

That curiosity shows up in the games, apps, and tools I build. Sometimes the result makes an everyday task easier. Sometimes it sends a rover to another planet. Both seem like reasonable uses of my time.

My day-to-day work has shaped a practical approach to solving problems. My personal projects give that approach room to experiment, learn new skills, and have some fun along the way.
